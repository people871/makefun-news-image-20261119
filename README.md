GTA VI opens Vice City after a 13-year wait; the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin
trilateral meets in Shenzhen; the US and Iran exchange fire again in the
Gulf; and Cyclone Akvan bears down on India's east coast — with the date
and one short caption per story. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library
only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, deterministically, on
every run.
